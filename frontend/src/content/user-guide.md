## Clocking in and out

1. Open **Home**, choose a project and a task, and set the entry to billable or non-billable (it starts from the project default).
2. Select **Clock in**. A running entry starts at the current time.
3. You can only be clocked in once. A second clock-in is refused until you clock out.
4. Select **Clock out** to close the entry. Its duration, net of breaks, is added to that day of your timesheet.
5. If you forgot to clock out on an earlier day, Home shows an alert the next time you open it. Enter the correct end time and save. An end time that is not after the start, or is in the future, is rejected.

## Taking breaks

1. While an entry is running, select **Start break**. Select **End break** when you are back.
2. Each break is saved with its start and end times and is subtracted from the entry.
3. Only one break can run at a time.
4. If you clock out during a break, the break ends at the clock-out time.

## The weekly timesheet

1. The week runs Monday to Sunday. Use the arrows to move between weeks.
2. Add a row, choose a project and task, and type hours in the day cells.
3. Each day must be between 0 and 24 hours in total. Invalid values are rejected and nothing is saved.
4. Entries you clocked appear on the correct days next to hours you typed, and daily and weekly totals update.
5. Each row is billable or non-billable. Rows with different billable settings are listed separately.

## Editing and deleting entries

1. Change the day, hours, project, task or billable flag of an entry and save. Totals recalculate.
2. A running entry cannot be edited or deleted until you clock out.
3. Delete an entry after confirming. It is removed from your timesheet and totals, together with its breaks.
4. You can only see and change your own entries.

## The dashboard

1. Home shows your current week: total hours, hours per day, and the billable and non-billable split.
2. Running entries are not counted until you clock out.
3. With no entries you see zero hours.
4. All times are shown in the time zone on your profile, including across daylight-saving changes.
